## ACT 1: THE CRISIS (0:00 – 3:30)

### Scene 1 — Conference Room, Friday 3 PM (0:00 – 1:00)
**Visual:** Sleek modern office. Three people: ALEX (agency owner, 35), SARAH (content lead, 28), DAVID (stressed junior, 23).

**Client voice on phone (speakerphone):**
> "Alex, I need to be honest. We're paying you $18,000 a month, and our competitor just put out 40 videos this month. You gave us 6. Six. We're switching to another agency."

**Long silence. The call ends.**

**Alex (quietly):** "How many did they publish?"

**Sarah (defeated):** "Forty-three. Across YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn."

**David:** "Their whole team is three people. How are three people making 43 pieces of content?"

**Alex looks at the window. City outside.**

**Alex (VO):** "We had 12 people. We were losing to a team of 3. That weekend, I had to figure out why."

### Scene 2 — The Audit (1:00 – 2:30)
**Visual:** Alex at laptop at home, Saturday morning. She's mapping out their current workflow on a whiteboard. Split-screen animation shows each step.

**Alex (VO):**
> "Our workflow looked like this..."

**Animated whiteboard diagram appears:**

```
[Research] → 6 hours/video (manual Google, trend hunting)
     ↓
[Brief writing] → 2 hours
     ↓  
[Script] → 4 hours (writer, edits, revisions)
     ↓
[Thumbnail design] → 3 hours (Canva, back and forth)
     ↓
[Compliance review] → 1 hour (lawyer for music, images)
     ↓
[Upload + scheduling] → 45 min per platform
     ↓
TOTAL: ~17 hours per video
× 6 videos = 102 hours/month
```

**Alex:** "102 hours. Per month. Just for 6 videos. And the competitor was doing 43."

**Beat.**

**Alex:** "They had to be using AI. But not just ChatGPT."

### Scene 3 — The Discovery (2:30 – 3:30)

**Alex clicks. Reads. Her expression shifts from skeptical → curious → excited.**

**Alex (VO):** "The post had 400K impressions. The creator described a pipeline that sounded too good to be true. I signed up. Immediately."
